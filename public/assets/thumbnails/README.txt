================================================================
  PLACE YOUR YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL IMAGE FILES IN THIS FOLDER!
================================================================

1. Copy and paste your thumbnail JPG or PNG images into this directory:
   Path: d:\website 2\public\assets\thumbnails\

2. Example filenames you can save here:
   - thumb1.jpg
   - thumb2.jpg
   - gaming.png
   - tech.jpg

3. Then open `d:\website 2\lib\portfolioData.ts` and set the imageUrl:
   imageUrl: '/assets/thumbnails/thumb1.jpg',

Your portfolio website will instantly display your thumbnail image!
